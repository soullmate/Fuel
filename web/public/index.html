👟 Connect to Your Steps

The activity slider above is a rough estimate. By connecting your step counter or fitness tracker, FUEL can replace the estimate with your actual daily movement.

More steps → higher activity multiplier → more calories. Rest days automatically scale back. No more guessing.

Works with Garmin, Apple Watch, Fitbit, Oura, Whoop, Samsung, and most fitness trackers.

Your agent can set this up by connecting to your wearable's API or health data.

🔄 What is Fuel Cycling?

Your agent combines different types of nutrition in feast and fast cycles, synced to your weekly, monthly, or any multi-day schedule.

Instead of eating the same targets every day, your body cycles through phases that optimise energy, recovery, and metabolic flexibility:

🍫 Buffer — high carb refeed
🌙 Pre-Fast — winding down
✨ Fasting — full reset
🥑 Keto — fat-adapted
🌿 Reintro — gentle carbs
⚡ Refuel — ramping up

Each day gets unique macro targets calculated from smooth curves — no two days are exactly the same.

When off, FUEL tracks simple daily targets based on your TDEE with a balanced macro split.

⌚ Wearable Sync

Connects to your fitness tracker (Apple Watch, Garmin, Fitbit, etc.) to automatically adjust your daily calorie and macro targets based on real activity data.

Instead of estimating activity level manually, your actual steps, heart rate, and exercise sessions feed directly into your targets — so hard training days get more fuel and rest days scale back.

Your agent can configure this with your wearable's API credentials.

📈 Glucose Monitor

Integrates with continuous glucose monitors (CGMs) like Libre or Dexcom to adapt your carb targets based on real-time blood sugar response.

If your glucose spikes after certain meals, the system learns and adjusts your carb ceiling. During stable glucose periods, it may allow more carbs — personalising your nutrition to your metabolism.

Your agent can connect this to your CGM data source.

🧪 Ketone Monitor

Tracks your blood or breath ketone levels to measure how deeply you're in ketosis and adjust targets accordingly.

During keto phases, it verifies you're actually producing ketones and can tighten or relax carb limits based on your readings. It also helps optimise the transition between keto and carb phases.

Your agent can set this up with your ketone meter data.

💪 Workout Sync

Automatically detects training vs rest days and sets different macro targets for each.

Training days get higher carbs and calories to fuel performance and recovery. Rest days shift toward lower carbs and moderate fat. Works alongside cycling mode for even more precise daily targets.

Your agent can configure this with your training schedule or gym app.
